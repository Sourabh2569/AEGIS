# Research Status Language

Internal to user-facing labels:

- `READY_FOR_ACTUAL_RESEARCH`: Ready for research.
- `READY_WITH_CAUTION`: Ready with caution.
- `BLOCKED`: Research blocked.
- `PROVIDER_SETUP_REQUIRED`: Provider or licensed file required.

Forbidden labels in this sprint:

- Paper ready.
- Live ready.
- Validated strategy.
- Investment recommendation.
